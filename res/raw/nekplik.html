How to arrange a drive

A Movies and a Series folder at the top of the drive, with one folder per movie or series. Only title and description are needed in info.json.

Movies/
  Inception/
    movie.mkv          the video (any name)
    poster.jpg         2:3, about 600×900
    background.jpg     16:9, about 1920×1080
    info.json
    movie.en.srt       subtitles (optional)
Series/
  The Office/
    poster.jpg, background.jpg, info.json
    Season 1/
      01 Pilot.mp4
      01 Pilot.json    {"title": "...", "description": "..."} (optional)
      01 Pilot.jpg     episode picture (optional)
