Files

What's on the TV and any USB drive in it. Drop files here to send them to the TV.

Files are off

So nothing on the network can see or change what's on the TV, this page stays off until you turn it on. On your computer, in the Telebisyon folder, run:

sh files.sh <TV_IP>

It's a good idea to set a PIN for these pages too: sh pin.sh <TV_IP> 1234
