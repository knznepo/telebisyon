Call the TV

Your camera and microphone show on the TV straight away, over whatever is on. Nobody has to answer. The TV can't see or hear you back.

One more step

Browsers only let a page use your camera over a secure connection, so calls open on the TV's secure address. The first time, your browser warns that the connection isn't private, because the TV made its own certificate.

  1. Tap Continue below.
  2. On the warning, tap Advanced (or Show details), then Proceed (or visit this website).
  3. Allow the camera and microphone when asked.
